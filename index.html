About Our Committee

Sri Sri Maa Durga Puja Committee celebrates Durga Puja with profound devotion, cultural traditions, community participation, and the divine blessings of Maa Durga.

Based in the vibrant heart of Jugsalai Fatak, our committee was proudly established in 2024 with the vision of uniting devotees, preserving age-old traditions, and spreading spiritual joy throughout the community.
